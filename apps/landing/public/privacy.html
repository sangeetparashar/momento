Privacy Policy

How Momento collects, uses, and protects your information

Effective Date: May 19, 2026 · Last Updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Momento Network ("Momento," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Momento mobile application and related services (the "Service"). Momento is a location-based platform for content creators that incorporates Solana blockchain wallets via the Privy SDK.

By using Momento, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

01 Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign in to Momento using Privy's Google OAuth flow, we receive your email address, name, and Google profile photo from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

Wallet information

Momento uses Privy to provision embedded non-custodial Solana wallets on your behalf. Your wallet's public address is associated with your Momento account. Private keys are managed by Privy's secure key management infrastructure; we never receive, store, or have access to your private keys.

Location data

Momento is a location-based service. With your permission, we collect your device's approximate location to:

  • Show you content from creators within a 5-100 km radius of your current location
  • Allow you to publish geotagged posts at your current location (only if you choose to post)

Location data is stored only when you actively use the geofeed or publish a geotagged post. We do not track location in the background.

Content you create

Any posts, comments, likes, follows, or other content you create on Momento is stored on our servers and shown to other users in accordance with the Service.

Device information

We collect a device push-notification token (if you grant notification permission) so we can deliver push alerts. We also receive basic device metadata (operating system, app version, device model) for diagnostic and reliability purposes.

KYC information (creators only)

If you apply to become a verified creator on Momento, we collect additional identity verification information required by our Know Your Customer (KYC) provider, which may include name, date of birth, government-issued ID, and proof of address. This information is processed by our KYC partner and used solely for identity verification and regulatory compliance.

Usage and analytics

We collect basic usage analytics (screens viewed, actions taken) to understand how Momento is used and to improve the Service. This data is aggregated and not used to identify individual users.

02 How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the Service: deliver feeds, render posts, process interactions (likes, follows, comments)
  • To authenticate you: via Privy and Google OAuth
  • To enable wallet functionality: via Privy's embedded wallet infrastructure
  • To send notifications: push alerts about new follows, likes, comments, and platform updates
  • For safety and abuse prevention: detect and prevent fraud, spam, and Terms of Service violations
  • To comply with legal obligations: respond to lawful requests from authorities
  • To improve the Service: debug, analyze trends, ship new features

04 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following situations:

  • With other users: Your profile (name, photo, bio), posts, comments, follows, and likes are visible to other Momento users in accordance with the Service's design.
  • With service providers: The third parties listed in Section 03 process data on our behalf to operate the Service. They are bound by contract to use data only for the purposes we direct.
  • For legal reasons: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Momento, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: If Momento is acquired or merges with another entity, your information may transfer as part of that transaction.

05 Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including TLS encryption in transit, secure password handling via Privy, and access controls on our backend. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately (see Section 09).

06 Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws), you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent previously granted (e.g. revoke location permission in iOS Settings)
  • Data portability — request a machine-readable copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at brian@mylz.run. We will respond within 30 days.

Account deletion: You can delete your Momento account at any time from within the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account). Account deletion removes your posts, comments, likes, follows, and KYC records from our systems within 30 days. Your Solana wallet itself is managed by Privy; deleting your Momento account does not delete your wallet on the Solana blockchain.

07 Children's Privacy

Momento is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA / UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

08 International Users

Momento is operated from Canada. If you use the Service from outside Canada, you understand that your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in Canada and other countries where our service providers operate. Some of these jurisdictions may have data protection laws different from those in your country.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you within the app, by email, or by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
